## 🤔 What is Xal's Roster Roundup?

Blizzard's guild roster tells you who's in the guild. Roster Roundup tells you everything else.

It gives a Guild Master (or any officer) real tools for running a guild: notes on every member, alts linked to their mains, guild applications you can act on, a recruiting desk that finds and whispers new players for you, a log of everything that happens, and officer tools for the jobs that eat your evenings — all inside the game, in one window.

---

## ⚡ Key features

✅ **Recruitment, done properly** — three pages: Search, Ads, and Stats. Scan for unguilded players by level, class, race, and zone, then whisper them one candidate at a time. Your guild's current class mix shows right on the class filter, so you can see the gaps. Players you simply run past (nameplate, target, mouseover) can join your candidates too.
✅ **Recruit Replies box** — a small chat box that collects the whispers you sent and every answer. It pops up when someone writes back, and a right-click on their name gives you the game's own menu (Invite to Guild and all).
✅ **Blacklist that keeps up** — automatic opt-out detection, temporary blocks, and a "never contact again" entry right in the player right-click menu for the replies it misses.
✅ **Recruitment ads** — queue your General and Trade posts and send them one at a time, with Stats on how many people you recruited by day, week, and month.
✅ **Guild Master and Officer pages** — a roster overview, new members to review, rank changes made by other officers, a Ban List, promotion rules, strikes, check-ins for members who've gone quiet, and Message of the Day and guild info editing.
✅ **Officer sync** — when two officers with the addon are online, shared data syncs by itself. No exporting, no importing.
✅ **Applications** — review pending Guild Finder applicants and Accept, Decline, or Whisper them, with an optional automatic whisper on accept.
✅ **Member profiles and Mains / Alts** — Note, Officer's Note, custom notes, and dates for anyone the addon didn't see join. A star marks a confirmed Main, and linked alts sit right next to it.
✅ **Activity Log** — every join, departure, promotion, demotion, level-up, note change, returning member, and banned-name rejoin, searchable and filterable, with a 7- and 30-day summary, who left most recently and what rank they held, and an export you can paste into a spreadsheet.
✅ **Backups** — a safety copy of your guild data about once a day, one-click restore, and Export / Import to move it between computers.
✅ **Floating alert icon and mouseover card** — the icon lights up for a pending application, an unreviewed member, or a rank change, and hovering a name in Blizzard's guild roster shows an instant profile card.
✅ **Six skins and your own colors** — pick a palette (Crimson, Ember, Bronze, Midnight, Indigo, or the light Parchment), then tune the accent, background, and any installed font, with size, outline, and shadow options.
✅ **Resizable window, Esc-menu settings, and a minimap button** — left-click opens the main window, right-click jumps to the Roster page.

## ⌨️ Slash commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/rrapps` | Prints a quick check of why guild applications are or aren't showing |
| `/rrreplies` | Shows or hides the Recruit Replies box |
| `/rrroster` | Prints what the game's roster call returns right now (`/rrroster reset` clears and re-reads your current guild's saved roster) |
