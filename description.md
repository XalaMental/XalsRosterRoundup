A full guild roster toolkit for Guild Masters and officers - member profiles, applications, recruitment, officer tools, and more, all in one window.

---

## 🤔 What is Xal's Roster Roundup?

Roster Roundup gives a Guild Master (or any officer) real tools for actually running a guild, instead of Blizzard's own bare-bones roster window. Track notes and custom notes per member, link alts to their mains, review and act on guild applications, recruit new members, keep an eye on who's gone quiet, and see a real activity log of who joined, left, got promoted, or got demoted - all without leaving the game.

---

## ⚡ Key features

- **Recruitment** - three pages: Search, Ads, and Stats. Scan for unguilded players by level, class, race, and zone, then whisper and invite them one candidate at a time. Posts your recruitment ads to General/Trade on a queue, keeps a blacklist (including players who opt out), and tracks how many people you recruited by day, week, and month.
- **Guild Master page** - a roster overview card, unreviewed new members, rank changes made by other officers, a Ban List, promotion rules, and Promote/Demote/Kick, gated on your character's real in-game rank permissions.
- **Officer page** - the same overview and officer tools for any officer, without the Guild-Master-only settings.
- **Officer tools** - members ready for promotion, strikes, inactive-member check-ins, and your own Message of the Day and guild info editing.
- **Officer sync** - when two officers with the addon are online, shared data syncs automatically. No exporting or importing.
- **Applications page** - review pending guild-finder applicants and Accept, Decline, or Whisper them, with an optional automatic whisper on accept.
- **Member profiles** - Note, Officer's Note, Custom Notes, and alts, right on the Roster page.
- **Mains / Alts** - a star marks a confirmed Main, right-click a name to set one, and linked alts show right next to it.
- **Manual date entry** - set Date Joined, Promoted, or Birthday by hand for anyone the addon didn't personally see join or get promoted.
- **Activity log** - every join, departure, promotion, demotion, level-up, note change, returning member, and banned-name rejoin, searchable and filterable.
- **Floating alert icon** - lights up in a different color for a pending application, an unreviewed new member, or a rank change.
- **Mouseover panel** - hover any name in Blizzard's own Guild Roster window for an instant profile card.
- **Right-click menus** - invite and recruit actions right from player menus.
- **Per-guild Discord link** - your Guild Master sets your own guild's invite link.
- **Fully customizable look** - accent color, background, and any font from your installed fonts, with size, outline, and shadow options.
- **Resizable window, Esc-menu settings panel, and a minimap button** - left-click opens the main window, right-click jumps to the Roster page.
