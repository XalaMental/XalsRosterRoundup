# Xal's Roster Roundup

**Xal's Roster Roundup** is a World of Warcraft addon for Guild Masters and officers. It gives you real tools for running a guild - recruiting, member notes, alts, applications, an activity log, and officer tools - all in one window, instead of Blizzard's bare-bones roster.

## Key Features

*   **Recruitment:** Search, Ads, and Stats pages. Scan for unguilded players by level, class, race, and zone and whisper them one candidate at a time. The class filter shows how many of each class your guild already has, and players you simply run into (nameplate, target, mouseover) can become candidates too.
*   **Recruit Replies box:** A small chat box that collects the whispers you sent and every answer, and pops up when someone writes back. Right-click a name for the game's own player menu, including Invite to Guild.
*   **Blacklist:** Automatic opt-out detection, temporary blocks, and a "never contact again" entry in the player right-click menu.
*   **Recruitment ads:** Queue General and Trade posts, with stats by day, week, and month.
*   **Guild Master and Officer pages:** Roster overview, new members to review, rank changes made by other officers, a Ban List, promotion rules, strikes, check-ins for members who've gone quiet, and Message of the Day and guild info editing.
*   **Officer sync:** When two officers with the addon are online, shared data syncs automatically.
*   **Applications:** Review pending Guild Finder applicants and Accept, Decline, or Whisper them.
*   **Member profiles and Mains / Alts:** Note, Officer's Note, custom notes, dates set by hand, a star for confirmed mains, and linked alts shown next to them.
*   **Activity Log:** Joins, departures, promotions, demotions, level-ups, note changes, returning members, and banned-name rejoins - searchable and filterable, with a 7- and 30-day summary, a recent-leavers list, and an export for spreadsheets.
*   **Backups:** A safety copy of your guild data about once a day, one-click restore, and Export / Import.
*   **Alert icon and mouseover card:** A floating icon for pending applications, unreviewed members, and rank changes, and a profile card when you hover a name in Blizzard's guild roster.
*   **Skins and customization:** Six skins (Crimson, Ember, Bronze, Midnight, Indigo, Parchment), plus accent color, background, and any installed font.
*   **Window and settings:** Resizable window, an Esc-menu settings panel, and a minimap button.

## Slash commands

*   `/rrreplies` - show or hide the Recruit Replies box.
*   `/rrroster` - print what the game's roster call returns right now; `/rrroster reset` clears and re-reads your current guild's saved roster.
*   `/rrapps` - a quick check of why guild applications are or aren't showing.
