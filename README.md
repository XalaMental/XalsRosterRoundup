# Xal's Roster Roundup

**Xal's Roster Roundup** is a World of Warcraft addon built for guild officers and Guild Masters to actually keep track of who's who in the guild - mains, alts, professions, and why they're around - without digging through a spreadsheet or trying to remember it all.

## Key Features

*   **Live Guild Roster Sync:** Automatically pulls in every real guild member the moment you open the Guild Roster panel - no manual entry required to get started.
*   **Main/Alt Tracking:** Link alts to their main character with a live-filtering autocomplete, and swap which character is "the main" at any time (season to season, role to role) without losing any of that character's own data.
*   **Profile Cards:** Nickname, up to two professions (each with autocomplete against the real profession list), and tag-based "reason they're here" (Raid, Dungeon, Achievements, Mounts, Community).
*   **New Member Tracking:** Brand-new members are flagged in orange until you've opened their profile, so nothing slips through unreviewed.
*   **Guild Master Tools:** Private notes per member, an Officer list, and (Guild Master only) real in-game Promote/Demote/Kick actions - each behind a confirmation popup, Kick behind two.
*   **Communication Page:** Any member can quickly whisper the Guild Master or any Officer straight from the Options window.
*   **Customizable Look:** Accent color, background color/opacity, and body text font/size/outline, all from the Options window.
*   **In-Game Settings Panel:** A full standalone Options window - Home, Communication, Text, Display, Guild Master, and Data (storage scope) sections.
*   **Minimap Button:** Left-click opens Options, right-click opens the Guild Roster panel directly.
