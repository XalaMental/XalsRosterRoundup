Simply Sans Bold is bundled here as CustomFont.ttf and is the branded
title font for this addon's windows. Fira Sans Medium is bundled as
FiraSans-Medium.ttf and is the default body-text font. Both are licensed
under the SIL Open Font License (SimplySans-LICENSE.txt /
FiraSans-LICENSE.txt), which permits bundling with software.

The title font is fixed and not user-changeable. The body font can be
changed from the Options window's Text page (left-click the minimap
button, then the "Text" section) - pick a different font family/size/
outline there; Fira Sans Medium is just the default until you do.
